title: "Verify Account"
description: "Complete account verification to unlock higher rate limits and premium model access."
---

Hapuppy uses a lightweight verification step to prevent abuse and gate access to high-cost upstream models (notably Claude Opus and some image/video models). New accounts can use the free tier and most basic models immediately; verification unlocks the full catalog.

## Why verify?

- **Prevent abuse** of high-cost upstream APIs by automated scrapers and shared keys
- **Unlock premium models** (Claude Opus, o1, Midjourney, Sora, etc.)
- **Raise rate limits** beyond the default 100 RPM (case-by-case)
- **Enable refund eligibility** on credit pack purchases

## What you get without verification

- ✅ Free tier: 100K daily credits
- ✅ Basic chat models (Haiku, GPT-4o-mini, DeepSeek, Gemini Flash, etc.)
- ✅ Embeddings, transcription, moderation, basic image generation
- ❌ Premium chat models (Opus, o1, o3)
- ❌ Premium image/video models (Midjourney, Sora 2, etc.) — may also require [Claude Apply](/guides/claude-apply) for Claude-specific models

## Verification steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Sign in to the dashboard">
  Open [hapuppy.com/dashboard](https://hapuppy.com/dashboard) and sign in with the email you registered.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the Verification panel">
  In the sidebar, click **Account → Verification**.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit required information">
  Provide the information requested. Typically this includes:

  - **Email confirmation** — click the link in the email we sent
  - **Phone or messenger** — receive and enter a one-time code
  - **Payment method** — link a credit card or PayPal (a small temporary hold may be placed and immediately released)

  <Note>
    Hapuppy does not perform invasive KYC. Verification is intended to confirm you are a real, unique user — not to collect identity documents.
  </Note>
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for approval">
  Most verifications complete within minutes. Edge cases (e.g., regional restrictions, duplicate signals from a shared IP) may take up to 24 hours of manual review.

  You will receive an email when your account is verified.
</Step>

<Step title="Use unlocked models">
  Once verified, the full model catalog is available immediately. You can now:

  - Send chat requests to premium models (`claude-opus-*`, `o1`, `o3`, `gpt-5`)
  - Apply for Claude high-tier model access — see [Claude Apply](/guides/claude-apply)
  - Request rate limit increases via support
</Step>

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="My email confirmation link expired">
  Confirmation links expire after 24 hours. Sign back in and click **Resend confirmation email** from the verification panel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm not receiving the SMS / messenger code">
  Common causes:
  - Carrier filtering — try requesting the code 2–3 times with a 1-minute gap
  - Wrong country code — make sure the dropdown matches your phone's actual region
  - Messenger restrictions in your region — use the email-only path instead

  If it still fails, contact [support@hapuppy.com](mailto:support@hapuppy.com) with your account email and last 4 digits of the phone number.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My payment method was rejected">
  - Some virtual / prepaid cards are blocked by our payment processor (Creem). Use a standard credit card or PayPal.
  - Make sure billing address country matches the card-issuing country.
  - International cards: check that international transactions are enabled on your card.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Verification is stuck in 'Pending review' for over 24 hours">
  Email [support@hapuppy.com](mailto:support@hapuppy.com) with your registered email and "verification stuck" in the subject. Manual reviews are usually resolved within one business day.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I got 'Account already verified on another email'">
  Hapuppy enforces one verified identity per person. If you have an old account you no longer access, contact support to merge or transfer.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

- Verification data (email, phone, payment method) is stored encrypted and used only for fraud prevention and account recovery.
- We do **not** sell, share, or use this data for advertising.
- You can request deletion of verification data after account closure — though some records may be retained for legal/financial obligations (typically 6–12 months).

<Info>
  Need access to premium **Claude** models specifically? After verification, you'll still need to submit a separate request — see [Claude Apply](/guides/claude-apply).
</Info>
