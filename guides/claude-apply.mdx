---
title: "Claude Apply"
description: "Request access to premium Claude models (Opus, Sonnet high-tier)."
---

Anthropic places extra capacity controls on its top-tier Claude models (notably **Claude Opus**). To prevent these limited slots from being consumed by spam or abusive workloads, Hapuppy gates them behind a short application step. Once approved, the models become available immediately via the same `/v1/chat/completions` endpoint — no separate API.

## Who needs to apply?

| Model | Application required? |
|---|---|
| `claude-haiku-*` | No — available to all (free tier included) |
| `claude-sonnet-*` | No — available after [account verification](/guides/verify-account) |
| `claude-opus-*` | **Yes** — submit a Claude Apply request |
| Future Claude high-tier models | Yes — same flow as Opus |

## Prerequisites

Before applying, you must:

1. Have a **verified account** — see [Verify Account](/guides/verify-account)
2. Hold a **Standard or Pro** credit pack (Lite is generally not accepted for Opus)
3. Have a brief description of your intended use case ready

## How to apply

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the application form">
  In the dashboard, navigate to **Models → Claude Apply**.
</Step>

<Step title="Describe your use case">
  Provide a short summary (a few sentences) of:

  - What you're building (production app, research, agent, internal tool, etc.)
  - Expected request volume per day
  - Why Opus specifically — what Sonnet can't do for your task

  <Note>
    You do not need to share proprietary details. A general description (e.g., "code review agent for an internal team of 20 developers, ~5K requests/day") is sufficient.
  </Note>
</Step>

<Step title="Submit and wait">
  Most applications are reviewed within **1–3 business days**.

  Common approval signals:
  - Verified account in good standing
  - Reasonable use case (not "I want to test everything")
  - Sufficient credit pack tier

  Common rejection signals:
  - Brand-new unverified account
  - Vague or evasive use case description
  - Past abuse signals (banned key reuse, etc.)
</Step>

<Step title="Receive approval">
  You'll receive an email confirming access. Opus model IDs (`claude-opus-*`) will start working immediately — no client-side change required.

  ```python Python
  # Before approval: returns 403 model_access_denied
  # After approval: works normally
  resp = client.chat.completions.create(
      model="claude-opus-4-20250522",
      messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Hello!"}]
  )
  ```
</Step>

</Steps>

## What if I'm rejected?

You'll receive an email with the specific reason. Common next steps:

- **"Insufficient plan"** — upgrade to Standard or Pro, then re-apply
- **"Account not verified"** — complete [Verify Account](/guides/verify-account) first
- **"Use case unclear"** — re-apply with more detail (give realistic volume, specific Sonnet shortcomings)
- **"Recent abuse signals"** — contact [support@hapuppy.com](mailto:support@hapuppy.com) for review

You can reapply anytime — there's no cooldown on rejection.

## Limits after approval

| Limit | Default |
|---|---|
| Requests per minute | 100 (shared with all other endpoints) |
| Daily Opus token budget | Subject to credit balance × Opus multiplier (~5×) |
| Concurrent streams | 5 (contact support to raise for production use) |

If you need higher limits for production traffic, email [support@hapuppy.com](mailto:support@hapuppy.com) with traffic projections and we'll provision a custom quota.

## Cost reminder

Opus is the most expensive Claude model — typically ~5× the credit multiplier of Sonnet. A 2K-token call to Sonnet (~2K credits) is the same cost as a 400-token call to Opus. Make sure Opus is the right tool before committing your vault to it; see the [Credits & Pricing](/guides/credits#cost-optimization-tips) tips.

<Info>
  Approval is per account, not per API key. If you generate a new key, your Opus access carries over automatically.
</Info>
